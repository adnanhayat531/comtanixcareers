## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a refined, high-end corporate identity tailored for an enterprise Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) and IT services firm catering to US-based clientele. It conveys unwavering reliability, institutional security, architectural clarity, and forward-looking technological proficiency. The interface positions careers and partnerships within an aspirational, global standard of excellence.

The visual direction follows a **Corporate / Modern Tech** design philosophy infused with subtle architectural precision:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Built entirely on a rich spectrum of oceanic and crystalline teals against soft mist backdrops, strictly eliminating pure black `#000000` to prevent harsh visual clipping and preserve a sophisticated, human-centric tone.
- **Precision Typography:** Modern geometric display hierarchy using Sora paired with the structural readability of Public Sans for institutional clarity.
- **Tactile Refinement:** Subtle linear micro-borders and soft, tinted ambient elevations that provide depth while maintaining pristine data density.

## Colors

The color system is exclusively tuned for light mode, emphasizing cleanliness, openness, and calm authority. Pure black is strictly prohibited across all surfaces, text, borders, and shadows.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#49A4AE`):** Vibrant mid-teal used for primary call-to-actions, active indicators, and high-impact UI triggers.
- **Secondary (`#2A7A85`):** Deep oceanic teal used for elevated brand accents, button hover states, prominent headers, and high-contrast structural elements.
- **Tertiary (`#E6F3F5`):** Soft, ice-tinted teal wash applied to interactive hover states, selected card backgrounds, chips, and table highlights.
- **Neutral (`#1E3A3F`):** Deep petrol-slate neutral acting as the primary typography color, dense iconography baseline, and high-emphasis structural border tone.
- **Canvas Base (`#F3F9FA`):** Ambient background tinted with a cool, refreshing cast to soften full-screen eye strain.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white reserved for elevated cards, modal dialogs, and interactive input fields.

### Accent & Functional Accents
- **Border Subtle (`rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.2)`):** Delicately outlines cards, containers, and table partitions.
- **Muted Text (`#557A80`):** Secondary informational labels and supporting meta descriptions.

## Typography

The typographic system balances the geometric innovation of **Sora** for headings with the structured readability of **Public Sans** for functional interfaces, dashboards, and editorial narratives.

- **Headings & Titles (Sora):** Characterized by distinct geometric shapes, slight squareness, and generous apertures. Used to deliver high-level impact across executive summaries, corporate announcements, and job vacancy headers.
- **Body & Functional Text (Public Sans):** A neutral, highly legible sans-serif engineered for digital interfaces and dense information exchange. It prevents eye fatigue across career applications, documentation, SLAs, and technical specifications.
- **Microcopy & Data Badges:** Always rendered with `Public Sans` semibold or medium, utilizing slightly tracked letter spacing to optimize scanning efficiency in dense operations panels.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a structured 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing gracefully to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (32px), `margin` (32px), max layout container width of 1280px centered.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter` (24px), `margin` (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (16px), `margin-mobile` (20px).

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm is established via an 8-point base increment:
- Component paddings default to `space-md` (16px) for cards, and `space-sm` (8px) vertical by `space-md` horizontal for buttons and controls.
- Content groupings and section stacks use `space-lg` (24px) to `space-xl` (40px) to preserve breathing room and convey executive elegance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses cool, diffused ambient shadows tinted with the primary hue alongside crisp hairline borders. Harsh dark drop shadows are forbidden.

### Depth Architecture
1. **Canvas Level (Ground):** Tinted background `#F3F9FA`. No elevation.
2. **Surface Level (Layer 1):** Solid white `#FFFFFF` with hairline outline `1px solid rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.18)` and an ambient, low-profile tinted shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 58, 63, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.08)`.
3. **Floating & Hover Level (Layer 2):** Elevated interactive elements, modals, and hover states for career cards:
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(30, 58, 63, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.12)`.
   Borders shift from subtle to `1px solid #49A4AE`.
4. **Overlay / Drawer Level (Layer 3):** Dropdown menus, floating talent filters, and enterprise dialogs:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 58, 63, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is calibrated to **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)**, reflecting institutional precision, architectural sturdiness, and modern technological agility.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Form inputs, buttons, checkboxes, and micro tags.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Information cards, career listings, modal dialogue containers, and navigation bars.
- **Extra Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Hero feature callouts and presentation containers.
- **Hexagonal Geometric Accents:** Derived from the brand mark, subtle 6-sided geometric outlines may be employed for iconography enclosures, badge glyphs, and technical avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#49A4AE`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, 8px vertical / 20px horizontal padding, 4px border radius. Hover transitions to `#2A7A85` with a subtle lift shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(42, 122, 133, 0.25)`). Focus visible state uses a 2px offset ring of `#49A4AE`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, text `#2A7A85`, border `1.5px solid #2A7A85`. Hover fills with `#E6F3F5`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#1E3A3F`. Hover fills with `#E6F3F5` and shifts text to `#2A7A85`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** White background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.3)`, text `#1E3A3F`, placeholder `#557A80`. On focus: border shifts to `#49A4AE` with an outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Neutral unchecked state with 1.5px border `#49A4AE`. Checked state fills `#2A7A85` with white checkmark/dot. Never uses pure black outlines.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Career Tags:** Background `#E6F3F5`, text `#2A7A85`, border `1px solid rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.25)`. Font is `label-sm` with upper/camel casing for departmental categorizations (e.g., "Full-Time", "Cloud Architecture", "Healthcare BPO").

### Cards & Listings
- Base surface in `#FFFFFF`, with `1px solid rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.18)` and 8px border-radius. On career and service cards, hover adds a subtle translateY(-2px) accompanied by Layer 2 elevation and a border transition to `#49A4AE`.

### Data Lists & Tables
- Alternate row striping using `#F3F9FA` and `#FFFFFF`. Table headers are rendered in `label-md` with neutral text `#557A80` and subtle border bottom `1px solid rgba(73, 164, 174, 0.2)`. Row hover creates a gentle `#E6F3F5` wash.

### Client Trust & KPI Cards
- Specialized KPI stat panels utilizing high-contrast `#2A7A85` for metric digits, paired with a small geometric accent or hexagonal teal icon container.
